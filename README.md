# Odo
Odo is a personal vehicle tracker for your phone. It keeps your preventive maintenance service (PMS) schedule, fuel logs, GPS trips, and registration and license expiry reminders in one place. Everything is stored locally on your device, so it works offline with no account and no cloud.

Features

PMS and maintenance log with due-by-km or due-by-date reminders
Fuel log with km/L, monthly spend, and cost per km
Mechanic visit records with receipt photos
GPS trip tracking with a route map and live distance
OR/CR, license, and insurance expiry reminders

Tech stack: React Native, Expo, TypeScript, Expo Router, SQLite
